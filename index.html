Tooling & Torque Control Matrix

  • Driver Geometry: Standard flathead watchmaker screwdriver with a grip diameter of 5 to 6 mm.
  • Direct Fastening Tightening Torque: Apply 0.05 to 0.15 N·m to the housing screw (or 0.05 to 0.10 N·m for solid-state D-M9A(V) components).
  • Bracket System Fastening Torque: Apply 0.05 to 1.00 N·m directly to the bracket assembly screw (M2.5). Tighten by rotating approximately 90° beyond the structural point of resistance.
